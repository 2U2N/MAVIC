Strongly correlated MAVIC feature groups retained and removed during probe selection

Note. Each row lists a retained feature and every rejected feature with which it had an absolute Pearson correlation greater than .80. Audio/visual correlations were evaluated on 18,523 videos; linguistic additions were evaluated on 10,335 videos. The deterministic selection order prioritized duration and variability measures, and the canonical audio/visual vocabulary was retained when linguistic additions were selected. A rejected feature may appear in more than one row when it correlates strongly with multiple retained features.
